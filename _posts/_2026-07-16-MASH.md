My latest convert to darts enlightenment, [Jan Ratte](https://hopandschwein.wordpress.com/), has been hard at working tracking down steel-tip darts locations in the largely heretical soft-tip nation of Germany. He excelled himself, picking out several promising looking classic Berlin pubs that also had at least one board. My appetite for eckkneipe darts had been whetted by a DVD of [Heiko's Welt](https://letterboxd.com/film/heikos-welt/) dropped off on his last visit to Bristol. By the time I got to Berlin about a month later I was gagging for a Schultheiss and a throw. Out first stop was MASH, where we got stuck in on the Schultheiss, as well as a Nordberliner pils which gets a special mention in the film.

Mash is a small pub, with a smokey bar at the front and a small room at the back, I suspect only not smokey because we were the only ones in there. The board is the ever reliable Winmau Blade 6, in excellent condition. It had not been rotated, but the treble twenty bed was showing no signs of heavy use. This is possibly because it's only used occasionally, with the two soft-tip machines in the room. All of my knowledge of the Berlin darts scene comes from the film Heiko's Welt, where they only use the steel-tip board for the final of the showdown competition at the climax of the film, so I'll take that as gospel.

The oche left a little to be desired. On arrival it was blocked by a table, also pointing at the preference for soft-tip darts which had their own dedicated space. However, the barwoman was happy for us to play and gave us a hand moving the table and chairs out of the way. She even admonished me for shiftin the table back into position when we finished playing. It later transpired it was her first shift there, I suspect we were lucky not to be told to jog on. The line itself was clear, if a little worn. I didn't have my measuring string on me to check the length, but it seemed about right. From all of the darts memerobilia on the walls I suspect whoever set it up knew what the were doing.

The only missing element was a method of scoring. Fortunately, we had the latest version of the soon to be patented™ [Orange Lad darts](https://darts.orangelad.co.uk/) app to test, which was a huge success. I suspect the over-reliance on electronic machines to play darts has crippled the general ability to score with a chalkboard. In the wide picture below you can see a nifty stand, possibly for an ipad to display scores on the telly to the top left of the board. This could imply it's only used on special occasions, or they only play non-scoring games like round the clock.

We had a great time in MASH. It was exactly what I had been hankering after post Heiko's Welt. Even the faint wafts of cigarette smoke drifting in from the front room added to the atmosphere instead of being naueseating.
