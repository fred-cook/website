This will be a brief dartboard review as I have only thrown in the Knowle briefly. However, I always think my reviews will be short before surprising myself with how much I have to say about the merits of a raised oche. With [The George](https://fredcook.co.uk/2026/11/10/The-George) calling last orders early we snuck up the hill to the Knowle for a final pint. Normally I'd only review somewhere I'd played at least a few legs at, but it's far enough out of the way and up a big enough hill that I probably won't be back any time soon.

The board is a very basic Target World Champions round wire affair. It's not a set-up an actual darts world champion would be used to, at least in the modern era. It looked in great condition, I think because it's a relatively recent addition. There is nothing wrong with a round wire board per se, you just get more bounce outs, smaller beds to aim at and the wires tend to get a bit wobbly with use. The board was situated in a black cupboard, who's doors were lying on the table below it. If it's the [usual suspect](https://fredcook.co.uk/images/the-lazy-dog/board.jpg) of dartboard cupboards I don't think it will last long. It also doesn't offer much protection to the walls, especially at the six'o'clock and nine'o'clock positions. It had an LED light bar above, but no power supply for it on the visit.

The oche is a very simple affair, a piece of green tape stuck to a carpet. I guess the carpet could move over time with the passage of pub-goers across it, however, the pool table in the middle of it should hold it in place. I didn't measure the oche length but it seemed about right.

{% include figure.html
src="/images/the-knowle/oche.jpg"
alt="a piece of green tape with Pete's foot next to it for scale"
caption="The oche, modelled by Pete"
%}

There were no in-situ scoring options. I don't think there's a good spot near the board, unless you were to relegate the wall of dogs. Even then I think you'd be in the way of whoever was throwing as you chalked up your score. It wasn't an issue on this visit, we just played round the clock and threw at the bull for a bit before calling it a night.
